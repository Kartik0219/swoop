How are you covered?

Pick your cover and your treatment to see how it works and what to bring. Your choice goes straight into your booking request.

Your cover
Your treatment

Medicare Allied Health

You may be eligible for up to 5 bulk-billed visits in a calendar year. The condition must be chronic, and you need a referral from your doctor. We bill Medicare directly.

The clinic's Allied Health details are written for chiropractic. Please call (03) 9700 2145 to ask about massage before you book.

Bring

  • Your doctor's referral
  • Your Medicare card, shown at each visit
  • Time to sign the Allied Health form each visit

Private health insurance

We send the claim to your fund at the consultation (if your fund is online), so you only pay the gap. What you get back depends on your level of cover.

Mark gives you a receipt to send to your health fund to claim a rebate. What you get back depends on your level of cover.

Bring

  • Your health fund card
  • A card for the gap or the fee (EFTPOS available)

TAC

With a TAC claim number and prior approval, your visits may be covered in full. Once approval is given, every visit is billed to and paid by TAC.

Please call (03) 9700 2145 to check TAC cover for massage before you book.

Bring

  • Your TAC claim number
  • Prior approval from TAC

WorkCover

Chiropractic can be covered in full by WorkCover, so you are not out of pocket.

Remedial massage can be covered in full by WorkCover. A doctor's referral is usually for up to 10 visits, and you may need more than one referral.

Bring

  • Your claim number
  • Prior approval
  • The date of your injury
  • A doctor's referral

DVA gold card

Gold card holders may be eligible for bulk-billed chiropractic. Once we have your referral, every visit is bulk-billed to DVA.

Please call (03) 9700 2145 to check DVA cover for massage before you book.

Bring

  • A doctor's referral with your gold card and file number, and the referring doctor's details
  • Time to sign the DVA form after each visit
  • Your gold card

Paying yourself

Pay at the desk on the day. EFTPOS is available.

Bring

  • A card for EFTPOS

Who you'll see

Two practitioners, one clinic: chiropractic with Andrew and remedial massage with Mark.

Dr Andrew Braslis

Chiropractor · B.Sc. (Monash University), B.App.Sc. (Phillip Institute of Technology)

Practising for 25 years, 20 of them at this clinic. Andrew specialises in treating families and children, using biomechanical traction, soft tissue therapy, chiropractic mobilisation and activator methods.

A member of the Chiropractic Association of Australia. He sponsors and supports local clubs including the Doveton Soccer Club, the Endeavour Hills Cricket Club, the Lions Club and the YMCA.

Mr Mark Ladd

Remedial massage therapist · Dip. Health Sc. (Massage)

Over 16 years in massage, 11 of them at this clinic, with certificates in relaxation, sports and oriental massage. Mark works from relaxation through to deep tissue, and treats sports and work-related problems.

A member of the Australian Association of Massage Therapy. He has worked with the St Kilda Football Club for over 4 seasons, local junior sports teams, the World Masters Squash Tournament and the World Masters Games.

How long is a consultation?

A new patient's first consultation usually takes up to 30 minutes. Standard consultations normally take up to 15 minutes, more or less depending on your condition.

Will I need an X-ray?

After your first consultation the chiropractor decides whether an X-ray is necessary. If it is, you are referred for a spinal X-ray, bulk billed through Medicare.

Do you see children?

Yes. Andrew specialises in treating families and children.

Can I book a massage on its own?

Yes. Massage works alongside chiropractic treatment, or as a treatment on its own.

Do you have EFTPOS?

Yes, as well as electronic claiming for private health insurance if your insurer is online.
