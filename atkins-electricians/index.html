Flip a circuit

Pick home or business on the main switch, then flip a circuit to see what we do on it.

Rewiring

Many of Melbourne’s pre-1970s houses still have old, outdated cabling, which can be a real hazard. We rewire with up-to-date cabling that protects your family from dangerous electrical faults.

Power points and lighting

More power points or lighting? With your input we design and install it: garden and outdoor lighting, LEDs including strip lights, general power outlets and more.

Switchboard upgrades

Every home switchboard should have a safety switch. If you have an old porcelain fuse board, or circuit breakers without a safety switch, we upgrade it to a safer, up-to-date board.

LED upgrades

Almost any household light can be upgraded to LED. Compared to halogen downlights they are cheap to run and pose a much lower risk of fire, in styles to suit any room.

Oven and cooktop repairs

We repair faulty mechanisms on ovens, electric cooktops and upright electric oven and stove units, and can quote for a replacement unit when it’s needed.

Data and phone points

A new phone socket, or more data points for Wi-Fi, cable TV, security or another desk? We install all types of communication wiring and repair existing wiring.

Design and installation

Lighting and power for commercial spaces. We attend site, select the hardware and work directly with the people involved, such as the chairperson or landlord, from start to finish.

Testing and tagging

Testing and tagging of portable electrical appliances, as needed or as a regular scheduled service. Where repairs are needed we can often do them on the spot.

Maintenance, 24/7

Electrical emergency? We’re here 24/7, every day of the year. An urgent job at 3am or a faulty light fitting on a Wednesday, someone will answer the phone.

Data and communications

We install, upgrade and repair fittings for all types of data and communications systems, from a simple phone installation to upgrading a fit-out to Cat 6 cabling.

Exit and emergency lighting

Fire safety regulations require exit and emergency lights in commercial buildings to be tested every 6 months. We do the testing, maintenance and repairs, and quote for replacements.

Switchboards and meter enclosures

Many of Melbourne’s older commercial buildings still have porcelain fuse boards and worn-out meter enclosures. We have replaced hundreds of them and bring them up to today’s standards.

Three generations since 1951

Keith Atkins started the business in 1951. It carried on through his sons, John and Mark. John has since retired, and Mark now runs Atkins Electricians with his son Joel, with a team of electricians behind them.

We work across Bayside and the surrounding suburbs, on houses, units and commercial buildings. No job is too big or small, from general rewiring to commercial testing and tagging.

Our focus is customer service, with an emphasis on punctuality and cleanliness.

Our electricians are “A” grade, and the company is registered with Energy Safe Victoria, REC 26182.
