Residential air conditioning

A well designed system is the difference between a home you want to be in and one you want to get away from.

From a single reverse cycle split system for one room to whole-home heating and cooling, we calculate the heating and cooling load for the layout and orientation of your home first. We are not tied to any one manufacturer, so we recommend the system that suits you, whether that is energy efficiency or performance in any weather.

  • Design and installation for new and existing homes
  • Heating and cooling load calculated on every project

  • Modifications and alterations to existing systems

Commercial air conditioning

From one split system to multi-storey developments and green building upgrades, in buildings that are vacant or fully tenanted.

Each building is unique. We calculate the load for its layout, orientation and use, so the system meets your needs and Australian Standards. Our fleet of fully equipped service vehicles means most problems are solved on the spot.

Projects

  • New building HVAC design and installation
  • System upgrade or replacement
  • Chiller replacement and upgrade
  • Installation of VRV systems
  • Cooling tower upgrade and replacement
  • Building automation
  • 'Green' energy efficiency upgrades

Service

  • Breakdown service and repairs
