Adult ADHD

ADD was once thought to be a childhood condition that resolved in adulthood. We now know it is often lifelong.

Many people with ADD are not 'hyperactive', and may be more 'day dreamy'. It often runs in families, so when a child is diagnosed, a parent's ADD can go unrecognised.

We offer 2 appointments for the assessment of ADD, and recommend involving next of kin where possible. Management looks at lifestyle, adjusting to the diagnosis, family relationships and self-esteem, with ongoing psychotherapy support.

What if it's an emergency after hours?

If the clinic cannot be contacted, please contact your GP's clinic or attend the Emergency Department of your local hospital. Local public hospital psychiatric services also provide urgent assessment after hours through their Psych Triage services.

How long does the first part take?

It can take several appointments to understand your situation and explore treatment options. Treatment starts after the initial assessment, and the length of involvement depends on your circumstances.

What if hospital is needed?

Some patients and families find admission helpful for assessment and treatment. The Melbourne Clinic, The Albert Road Clinic and Beleura Private Hospital offer a wider team. Private health insurance, WorkCover or TAC is recommended.

Do I need to renew my referral?

Please check how long your referral lasts with your referring doctor and renew it before it ends, or Medicare won't provide a rebate. A specialist's referral lasts 3 months at most; a GP can give an indefinite referral.
