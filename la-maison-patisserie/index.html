Our story

Simple tradition, premium quality

La Maison Patisserie opened in Cheltenham in late 2011 and has become a well-known patisserie well beyond the neighbourhood.

Everything is handmade from scratch with premium ingredients by a professional pastry chef. Each cake is baked to order, never pulled from a stockpile of frozen cakes, and we're well versed in both buttercream and fondant work.
