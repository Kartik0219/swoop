Our staff

A counselling and psychology practice just outside the Frankston CBD, established 23 June 1988. Over 30 psychologists have worked at the centre since then.

Its psychologists and counsellors are members of associations such as the Australian Psychological Society, the National Association of Loss and Grief, the Australian Counselling Association and the Australian Hypnosis Society.

Michael Warner

Psychologist

B.A. Dip. Ed. Grad.Dip.Ed.Psych, Cert. Trauma & Grief, M.A.P.S.

Works with adults, adolescents and children. He has practised psychology for nearly 20 years, within the Department of Education, Youth at Risk, the Reach Foundation and private practice.

  • Depression
  • Anxiety
  • Couples
  • PTSD
  • Grief
  • Parenting

Psychologist

A primary teacher first, then six years as a counsellor and team manager at a community palliative care service. She has trained in hypnosis and play therapy, and works with all age groups.
