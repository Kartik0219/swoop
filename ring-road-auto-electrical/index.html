Our services

Auto electrical, start to finish

An electrical system isn't just a battery. It's a network linked by cables, and every connection has to be 100% to work efficiently.

Starting & charging

Starter motor and alternator replacement and rebuilds, including high-output units.

Fault finding

Short circuits, open circuits and component diagnosis.

EFI & scan tool

EFI fault finding and repair, with scan tool diagnosis.

Air conditioning

A/C repair and service, regas, leak detection and hose repair.

Lighting

Batteries
