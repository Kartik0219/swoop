Thinking about LPG?

Common questions, answered

Can my car be converted to LPG?

In most cases, yes. Call us with your make and model and we'll let you know.

Where does the gas tank go?

Sedans usually have it under the parcel shelf in the boot. Wagons usually in place of the spare wheel. Commercials and 4WDs mounted underneath.

Can I switch between gas and petrol?

Yes, you can switch fuels at idle or while driving, with a dash switch that also shows your LPG level.

Where do I fill up?

The LPG filler sits in the same compartment as your petrol filler.
