Children's dental

Child Dental Benefits Schedule, bulk billed

Eligible children aged 2 to 17 can receive dental services under the Child Dental Benefits Schedule. We bulk bill eligible services on the day, so there's no out-of-pocket cost for services covered under the scheme, up to the available limit. Just let us know you're eligible when you book.

We also offer fissure sealants, regular check-ups and early orthodontics for kids.

*Conditions apply. Contact your health fund to find out about your cover. Eligibility for the Child Dental Benefits Schedule is set by the Australian Government.
