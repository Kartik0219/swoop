Care for every age

From toddlers to braces at sixty-four. One practice for the whole family, with a plan built around each person, not just their teeth.

  1. Check-ups and cleans

    Think of it like a logbook service for your car: small problems are found sooner, before they become bigger and more costly. Most people need a check and clean every 6 to 12 months.

    Our standard fee is $195 before health fund rebates. HCF and Bupa members at Preston can have a check-up and clean with minimal to no out-of-pocket fees.

    Book a check-up

  2. Children's dentistry

    We love seeing kids from a young age, so the dentist becomes a familiar, friendly place. Eligible children aged 2 to 17 can be bulk billed through the Child Dental Benefits Schedule.

    Plan for your kids

  3. Invisalign and braces

    Dr Wang holds a master's and PhD in orthodontics; Dr Wendy has a Postgraduate Diploma in Digital Orthodontics and Invisalign accreditation. You choose between clear aligners and braces after a proper consultation, with a clear financial consent before anything starts.

    An orthodontic consult is $80.

    Book an orthodontic consult

  4. Fillings, crowns and implants

    Fillings and restorations, root canal treatment, crown and bridge, dentures and dental implants, with dentists whose special interests include endodontics, prosthodontics and implant dentistry.

    Book a visit

  5. Toothache or emergency

    Call the clinic nearest you and we'll find you a time. You can also text us on 0451 439 223.

    Call Box HillCall Preston

Dr Youping Wang trained at the West China School of Dentistry and was the first female PhD graduate in orthodontics in her field. She moved to Australia in 1991 and opened her first clinic in Preston in 2000. She loves treating kids and easing anxious patients.

Dr Wendy Hu, principal dentist, always looked up to her mum and followed her into dentistry. She graduated from the University of Queensland with first class honours, with special interests in Invisalign, children's dentistry, aesthetic dentistry and implants.
