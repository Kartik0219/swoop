About the clinic

Mount Medical Centre is a medical practice orientated towards family medicine, men's and women's health and preventative medicine. You'll find us at the Stud Road and Mountain Highway intersection in Wantirna, with ample parking and disabled access. Alongside our GPs we offer specialist and allied health services and onsite pathology. All routine childhood vaccinations are available, and travel vaccination information is available on request. If you need an interpreter, let us know when you book. Our doctors may visit regular patients at home if they are too ill to come in, depending on availability. After hours and on public holidays, call the Doctor Doctor locum service on 13 26 60. Book an appointment online.

Bulk billing

We are a Medicare Bulk Billing Practice. Eligible GP services are bulk billed. For anything else, our reception team can tell you the fee before you book.

Bulk billed

  • GP consultations
  • Chronic condition management plans and reviews
  • Mental health treatment plans
  • Health assessments

Fees may apply

  • Procedures like skin excisions
  • Diagnostic services like ECG and pathology
  • Allied health and other non-GP services
  • Patients without a Medicare number

Please cancel at least two hours before your appointment if you can't make it. Workcover, TAC and commercial driver medicals are billed privately.
