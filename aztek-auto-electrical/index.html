About Aztek

Your local auto electrician since 1994

Aztek Auto Electrical has been looking after drivers in Aspendale, Parkdale, Mentone, Mordialloc, Beaumaris and Black Rock since 1994. In 2012 we moved to a new, larger workshop on Balcombe Road, Mentone. We're 100% Australian owned and operated, and proud to sponsor local clubs, including the Parkdale Vultures Football Club.
