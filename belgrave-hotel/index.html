What defines us

We're a 100% independent, community-focused pub in Belgrave, passionate about supporting our locals and welcoming visitors to our beautiful corner of the Dandenongs.

Located just a short walk from Puffing Billy, boutique shops and the national park, our bar & bistro is proud to provide friendly service, quality food and the warm, authentic feel of a true local pub.

At our pub, you can enjoy live music from local bands, hearty meals and special events, all in a relaxed setting where great value, good company and memorable times come first.
