Therapy is a special kind of relationship. The person seeking help needs to feel safe and confident with their therapist.

When should I seek counselling or therapy?

Most often people seek counselling when life has reached some kind of crisis. Sometimes something bothers you but lurks in the background and seems easier to ignore. Shame, or fear of rejection, can also put people off.

Making time for yourself to understand a problem and find out how to deal with it is a sign of strength and courage, not weakness.

What happens in therapy?

It is shaped by what you are struggling with and who you are. At the start your therapist will want to understand what led you to seek help: relationships, work, family, your physical health, and important earlier experiences.

There may be things you don't want to talk about at a given time, and that is ok. You need to feel safe and in control of the process.

How much does it cost? Do you bulk bill?

Each practitioner sets their own fees, so ask when you call or enquire. All three list Medicare with a Mental Health Care Plan from your GP; see the programmes list above.

Can I have sessions by zoom or phone?

Christine offers in-person, zoom or phone sessions. Ask the others when you get in touch.

How do I get to you by public transport?

Get the train or bus to Frankston Railway Station, then the 770 bus along Beach Street, and get off at the Leonard St bus stop. The practice is diagonally opposite: cross the road and walk to the left about 50 metres.
