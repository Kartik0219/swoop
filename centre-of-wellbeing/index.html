Good to know

Health fundsCan I claim from my health fund?

Yes. All our therapists are registered with the major health funds, and you can claim on the spot through our HICAPS terminal. What you get back depends on your fund and your level of cover.

WorkCoverDo you take WorkCover and TAC?

Yes. WorkCover, TAC and other provider rebates are available, and many can be processed on site. For chiropractic we also see DVA patients and patients on a Medicare Chronic Disease Management plan.

ComfortDo I have to get undressed?

No. We can work through your clothes. You only undress to your level of comfort, you stay draped under towels, and we only uncover the area we're working on.

HoursCan I get a later or Saturday appointment?

After-hours appointments are available for many services. Book ahead for later times and Saturdays, as they are popular. If you can't make it, please give us plenty of notice.

ReferralsWhat if you can't help me?

If we feel we can't help, we have a network of other therapists we know and trust, some in house, that we can refer you to.
