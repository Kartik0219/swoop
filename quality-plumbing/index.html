Need your sewer connected?

If your property is in one of the suburbs affected by Yarra Valley Water’s Community Sewerage Program, you will receive a letter from Yarra Valley Water informing you that you’re ready to connect your property to the new sewer system.

Once their part is complete you will need a licenced plumber to connect your house to the new sewer service. Quality Plumbing has been conducting sewer connections and converting septic tank systems to both Pressure and Gravity systems for over 15 years.
