Dental services

General, cosmetic and implant dentistry for adults and children. Not sure which you need? Choose the enquiry type when you book and reception will help.

  • Preventive dentistry

    Dental health and nutrition, children's dentistry and gum disease.

  • Tooth coloured fillings

    Composite resin matched to your teeth, often done in a single appointment.

  • Teeth whitening

    Philips Zoom DayWhite and NiteWhite, with custom fitted trays for home.

  • Veneers and crowns

    Porcelain veneers, crowns, inlays and onlays, with digital scanning for crowns.

  • Smile makeover

    Whitening, fillings, gum lifts, implants, crowns and veneers, planned with you.

  • Dental implants

    Single tooth implants, implant bridges and overdentures, planned in 3D.

  • All-On-4

    A keen interest of Dr Sansil, along with complex implant restorations.

  • Bridges and dentures

    For tooth loss: bridges, dentures and denture replacement.

  • Root canal and wisdom teeth

    Root canal treatment and wisdom teeth, here at the practice.

  • Orthodontics

    Dr Powell is completing an extended program in orthodontics.

  • Grinding, jaw and snoring

    Tooth grinding (bruxism), TMJ / TMD and snoring.

  • Sleep / pain free dentistry

    Ask reception about it when you book your visit.

Meet the dentists

Five dentists work at the terrace. Tap a name to meet them, then book with them.

Dr Steven Speechley

Graduated from the University of Melbourne in 1987 and has spent the bulk of his career here at Knox Terrace Dental, with over 20 years experience in cosmetic and implant dentistry.

Member of the Australian Dental Association (ADA Victoria), the International Team for Implantology (ITI) and the Melbourne branch of the Seattle Study Group.

Away from work: family, music, gardening, food and sport, and hoping to see the Western Bulldogs win a premiership.

Book with Dr Speechley

Dr Eddie Sansil

Graduated from the University of Melbourne in 1998 and began practising at Knox Terrace Dental in 1999.

His courses have focused on dental implants, including a Master Course in Guided Bone Regeneration and Sinus Augmentation at the University of Berne, Switzerland, in 2012. Keen interests in surgical implant dentistry and complex implant restorations, including All On 4. Member of ADA Victoria and the ITI.

Away from work: travelling, walking, cars and motor sports.

Book with Dr Sansil

Dr Ross Powell

Bachelor of Dental Science, University of Melbourne, 1998. Has worked in practices across metropolitan Melbourne and the UK, and as a Technical Specialist for a dental manufacturer.

Offers digital scanning for patients having crown, bridge and implant treatment, and is completing an extended program in orthodontics.

Away from work: family time, and golf.

Book with Dr Powell

Dr Alex Ooi

Practising dentistry since 2003. He grew up in the Wantirna area and, after a few years in the CBD and inner Melbourne, joined Knox Terrace Dental in 2007.

Interested in all aspects of general dentistry, and in giving his patients a comfortable and informed experience.

Away from work: fitness, and swimming, which he once did at club and state level.

Book with Dr Ooi

Dr Irene Tan

Bachelor of Health Sciences in Dentistry and Master of Dentistry, La Trobe University. Before joining Knox Terrace Dental she treated patients in public and private clinics across metropolitan and regional Victoria.

She takes the time to listen to her patients' concerns, including people who find dental visits intimidating.

Away from work: the gym, swimming and time in nature.

Book with Dr Tan
