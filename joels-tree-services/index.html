Eleven services, one call.

Tree removal

Safe, efficient removal, no matter the situation, including tight access and complex removals. Dead and dying trees, roots damaging foundations, and trees in the way of an extension or new paving. No tree is too big or too small.

Tree pruning

Not all trees need removing to make them safe. We prune to Australian Standards: removing dead and hanging branches, weight reduction and crown thinning to improve the tree's structure.

Stump removal

Stumps are ground out with professional stump grinders, leaving a woody mulch in place of the stump. Machines for tight access areas, and larger grinders for large stumps.

Hedge trimming

An unruly or out of control hedge brought back into a manageable privacy screen, reduced or kept at the height you want, with regular upkeep if you need it.

Emergency storm damage

We do what is needed straight away to make it safe, then bring in any extra equipment to finish the job so further damage to your property is reduced. Fallen trees on driveways, roofs and access areas.

    Questions people ask

    Is my tree structurally sound and safe?

    We'd need to come and assess it. Each tree is different, and its structure, age and type can all decide whether it is still safe. Call us for some over-the-phone advice and to book an inspection.

    I need a tree removed urgently.

    For a storm or a fallen tree you won't have much time to wait. We have a rapid response time for urgent jobs. Call us or text us a picture of your tree and we'll take care of it as efficiently as possible.

    Do I need a permit to remove or prune this tree?

    It can depend. We can work with you and the local council so you only remove and tend to trees you're allowed to work on. Contact us first and we'll help you get the process started.

    What damage can this tree cause?

    Roots getting into your sewer and plumbing, or unsafe branches overhanging powerlines. If you're in any way unsure, call us and we'll advise on the best course of action.

    Can you identify the species of this tree?

    Yes, arborists are often asked to identify trees: for landscaping decisions, to understand a tree's needs, or to judge whether it suits its spot.

    Removals, pruning, stumps, hedges or a storm clean-up: tell us about your tree and we'll come and have a look.
