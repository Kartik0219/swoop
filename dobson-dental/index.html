Tricky question!

Cleans are usually done without local anaesthetic, unless gum disease exists. Most people would say cleans are uncomfortable more than they hurt.

We take baby steps, very carefully, and explain very slowly what we're doing each step of the way. No surprises, it's a promise.

Healthy gums don't bleed.

Bleeding means the gums are inflamed. This happens when plaque is left on teeth, and plaque is full of bacteria that irritate the gums.

If your gums bleed, book yourself in to see us pronto.
