About us

M1 Motors services and repairs European cars for Nunawading, the Eastern suburbs and a loyal customer base across Melbourne.

Our staff get the latest industry training and work with state of the art tools and equipment, so we can repair the latest BMW, Mercedes Benz, Audi, Volkswagen and Mini models.

We treat every customer with respect, keep you updated through the repair, and give honest advice. No job is too big or too small, from a minor service to an engine rebuild.
