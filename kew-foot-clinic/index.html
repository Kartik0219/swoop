About us

A friendly local team

Kew Foot Clinic is a 20 year old business based in Kew. We look after patients of all ages, from the very young to the elderly, and we're passionate about educating patients on preventing foot and lower limb joint problems.

Our team has worked together for nearly 10 years. Narelle has 20 years of podiatry experience and graduated from La Trobe University, Melbourne.
