Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Swoop Sites is run by Kartik Panikar, a sole trader based in Melbourne, Victoria (ABN 88 329 296 713). This page explains, in plain English, what personal information I collect through swoopsites.com, why, and how I look after it. I handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What I collect

  • Contact form: your name, business name, website, email or phone number, and your message.
  • Chat assistant: the messages you type, and any name, email or phone number you choose to share in the chat.
  • Email: anything you send to me by email, such as your business details, logo and photos.
  • Free website check: the website address you enter. The check looks at that site's public homepage only and the result isn't stored.
  • Technical information: like any website, my hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes your IP address and basic browser details to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. I use this only for security and spam prevention.

I don't use advertising trackers or analytics cookies on this site.

Why I collect it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a free homepage mockup or quote you asked for.
  • To provide the services you sign up for (building and looking after your website).
  • To keep records I'm required to keep, such as invoices.
  • To stop spam and misuse of the site.

I won't sell your personal information or share it for anyone else's marketing.

The chat assistant

The chat is an AI assistant, not a person. Your messages are processed by Cloudflare's AI service to generate a reply. If you share contact details in the chat, they're saved so I can get back to you. Please don't share sensitive information (such as health, financial or password details) in the chat.

Who helps me handle it

I use a small number of trusted service providers. Some of them may store or process information on servers outside Australia (including in the United States):

  • Cloudflare: website hosting, security, form and chat processing, and storage of enquiries.
  • Google (Gmail): enquiries are emailed to my business inbox.
  • Google Fonts: loads the font on this site (your browser contacts Google to fetch it).
  • Stripe: card payments, if you choose to pay by card (Stripe collects the card details; I never see them).

How long I keep it and how I protect it

Enquiries from the form and chat are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted automatically, unless you become a client (client and invoice records are kept as long as the law requires). Access is limited to me, accounts are protected with strong passwords, and the site uses HTTPS and modern security headers.

Your choices

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information I hold about you.
  • You can ask me to stop contacting you at any time, and I'll stop.
  • You can contact me without giving your name, but I won't be able to reply or prepare a mockup.

Questions or complaints

Email Kartikp.work@gmail.com and I'll reply within 30 days (usually much sooner). If you're not happy with my response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

I may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
