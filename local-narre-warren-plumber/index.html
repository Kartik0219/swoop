Asked most often

What causes a blocked drain? There are many causes, but the most common are foreign objects in pipes, a build-up of toilet paper and tree roots invading your plumbing. DIY methods may only fix the problem for a short time.

What is pipe relining? A 'no-dig' way to fix a burst, broken or cracked pipe: a resin forms a new layer of pipe. It is a fraction of the cost of replacement, your yard is not dug up, and its lifespan is just as long.

What does a gas fitter do? A gas fitter or gas plumber works on all gas appliances, hot water systems and the installation of gas lines and gas points. Incorrect installation can cause serious accidents, so it needs a qualified gas plumber.

I have no hot water? In most cases there is an issue with your water heater. Turn it off and check for any leaks or other signs of damage. If you still cannot find the issue, get in touch.

How do I know if I have a gas leak? A small leak may be undetectable, but people often smell a rotten egg type odour. Turn off your gas and electrical lines, get your family out of the property, call the emergency line and wait for the team.

How do I know if it is an emergency? A plumbing emergency is anything that may cause damage or harm to you or your property. It is always better to be safe than sorry, and the team is available 24/7.
