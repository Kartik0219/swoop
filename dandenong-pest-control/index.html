Termites

Termites cause more damage to Australian houses than fire, floods and storms combined.

Things to look for

  • Weak timber that breaks easily revealing wafer-thin layers. Skirting boards are often the first point of damage.
  • Changes in corner fascia under gutters, as termites are attracted to damp or moist areas.
  • Mud-like tubes or material around external footings and brickwork, or internal fittings like service pipes and electrical plugs.
  • Cracks or holes in timber or plaster with a fine dust residue.
  • Surrounding trees, wood piles and garden beds, up to a 50 metre radius around the home.

Pest termites in Victoria

Coptotermes
Widely distributed throughout Victoria and responsible for more than 80 per cent of termite damage to buildings.
Porotermes adamsoni
Victoria's largest species, sometimes called damp wood termite. May also be found in the dry foothills of Melbourne.
Nasutitermes and Schedorhinotermes
Less problematic in Victoria, or found only in the north of the state.
Green optionsDPC currently has green termite treatment options available.
Building, buying or renovating?Pre-construction termite management, pre-purchase inspections and annual termite inspections. The Australian standard recommends a timber pest inspection on all homes at least every 12 months.

Spiders

With an annual spider treatment, Dandenong Pest Control can help reduce webs and spiders around your home.

Good to know

  • Most spiders are nocturnal. When the light fades they leave their burrows or shelters to hunt or spin webs.
  • Treatments are best carried out in the warmer months, when spiders are most active.
  • There have been no human deaths caused by spiders since effective antivenom has been available for red back and funnel web spiders. Many bites can still cause painful reactions.
Large ones tooWebs are considered unsightly and spiders are known for being scary, especially large ones like huntsmen.

Cockroaches

Active cockroaches seen during daylight hours indicate an overpopulation.

Good to know

  • Of about 3,500 species worldwide, only about 10 are found in our dwellings.
  • They live in places like sewers and grease traps, then walk across kitchens and food handling areas. The most common disease they spread is likely salmonella (food poisoning).
  • They leave chemical trails that other cockroaches follow to food, water and hiding places.

Species

  • Australian
  • Brown Banded
  • Smokey Brown
  • Oriental
  • German
  • American
Housekeeping tipIt is important that there is no food or water left out overnight. Put lids on rubbish bins. Cockroaches can survive several months without food but will not last long without water.

Rodents

If the pest is left untreated, it can lead to further large-scale issues.

Common rodent species

Norway rat
Also known as the common rat, sewer rat or brown rat. Nests within walls and roof spaces. Life span 9–12 months.
Roof rat
Also known as the ship rat or black rat. Great climbers that can nest in buildings and roof voids.
House mouse
Also known as the field mouse. Can live indoors or outdoors in close proximity to humans.

What you can do

  • Make sure all inside bins have form-fitting lids. Empty food waste bins daily.
  • Dispose of green waste the same day as gardening.
  • Check around your property for cracks or holes that should be repaired.
Why it mattersRodents have incredibly strong incisor teeth. They can chew through plumbing, electrical wiring, furniture, carpets and even the building structure itself.

Ants

In Australia there are well over 4000 known species of ants, but only a handful are considered pests.

How they're treated

  • It is extremely important that the barrier treatment is a comprehensive one that attempts to isolate the nesting site from the food source.
  • Baits are used mostly where nest location and treatment is difficult or impossible. This may be a relatively slow procedure.

Species

  • Carpenter
  • Fire
  • Green
  • Flying
  • Bull
  • Black House
  • Argentine
  • Coastal Brown
  • Sugar
  • Pharoah
  • Ghost
  • Garden
  • Odorous House
Why it mattersAnts commonly scavenge in kitchens, food-handling areas and garbage bins, and their potential for transmitting diseases to humans should not be overlooked.

Wasps

European wasps are a pest because they are far more aggressive than native wasps.

Two you'll meet

European wasps
More aggressive than bees, and each wasp can sting multiple times. Large communal nests, often only visible as a small entrance hole in the ground, walls, ceilings, logs or trees.
Papernest wasps
Painful stings, but not as aggressive. They normally only attack if their nest is disturbed.
CarefulIn some cases, wasp stings can cause an allergic reaction (anaphylaxis).

Fleas

Fleas can lay dormant in previously occupied spaces, waiting for new tenants or owners.

Signs of fleas

  • Little specks hopping on your or your pet's bedding, or crawling on furniture, carpet or curtains.
  • Itching and small red bites on your feet, ankles and legs.
  • Your pet scratching more than usual. Part the fur around the ears and rump and look for fleas or tiny dark specks.

Main species in Australia

  • Cat flea
  • Dog flea
  • Human flea
Moving out?Is your lease coming to an end? Contact DPC to discuss an end-of-lease flea treatment.
TipPlace a few of the specks on a piece of lightly dampened white tissue. Flea faeces will stain the tissue with a ring of blood. Use gloves or wash your hands with warm soapy water.

Bees

Bees pollinate the planet! Be mindful of bees on your property if you suspect they have formed a hive.

Safety

  • It's important to stay calm.
  • There are over 1500 species of bees across Australia and the majority aren't aggressive unless they are provoked.
  • Do not attempt to remove a hive yourself.

Native bees

  • Yellow and Black Carpenter
  • Green Carpenter
  • Reed
  • Blue Banded
  • Teddy Bear
  • Leafcutter
  • Resin
Two-thirds of our foodBees pollinate two-thirds of our food production, so DPC wants to recognise how important they are.
