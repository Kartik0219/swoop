Services

Four ways we can help, in one clinic

Your practitioner will talk you through the options and work out what suits you best.

🦴

Chiropractic

Treatment of the joints, especially the spine, using manipulation or gentle mobilisation, combined with therapy for the surrounding muscles.

📍

Acupuncture & dry needling

Traditional acupuncture, dry needling, cupping and moxibustion, using single-use sterile needles.

💆

Remedial massage

Remedial, deep tissue, sports and relaxation massage, tailored to what you need on the day.

🤸

Myotherapy

Assessment and treatment of the soft tissues: muscles, tendons, ligaments and fascia.
