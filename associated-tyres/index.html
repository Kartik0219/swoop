Roadworthy

Selling, transferring or re-registering? Our VicRoads Licensed Vehicle Tester inspects passenger cars and light commercial vehicles for drivers in Moorabbin, Cheltenham, Bentleigh and around.

Booking the inspection

Call us on 03 9583 6452, send us a message, or come and see us at the workshop in Cheltenham.

On the day

Bring your car in with your driver's licence. A licensed vehicle tester assesses it, and if it passes, a Roadworthy Certificate is issued.

If it doesn't pass

We give you a list of what needs fixing and a quote for the repairs. Our technicians can do them, or a workshop of your choosing.

The re-inspection

Repairs and the re-inspection must be done within seven days of the failed inspection. If it passes, the certificate is issued.

Selling or transferring

You will also need an Application to Transfer Registration, from the VicRoads website.
