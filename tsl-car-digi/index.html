Australian owned

TSL Car Digi is an Australian owned company in Nunawading that makes custom built-in car GPS and DVD systems and other electronic products.

TSL is the importer and distributor of Dongguan City Yessun Electronic Co. Ltd, a car audio and sat nav maker in China whose brands are JYT, LISTEN and YESSUN.

Yessun makes more than 200 models for specific cars, each system configured to suit, for makes such as:

  • Honda
  • Toyota
  • Mazda
  • Mitsubishi
  • Nissan
  • Volkswagen

For your car, or your dealership

TSL supplies GPS systems to local car dealers and car audio shops for their aftermarket products.

Buying for your own car? Tell us the make, model and year. A dealer or car audio shop? Choose the second option, add your business details, and Tim will come back to you.
