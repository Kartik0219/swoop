FAQ

Questions people ask

Do I own my website and domain?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your name and your content is yours.

What's included in a care plan?

Hosting, HTTPS, daily backups, software updates, uptime monitoring and a set amount of edits each month. Higher plans add a monthly security report and email protection.

Do I have to get on calls?

No. Everything can be done by email, at your own pace. If you'd prefer a quick chat, that's fine too.

How long does it take?

Most sites go live in 1 to 2 weeks once we have your details.

Can you fix my existing website instead?

Often, yes. Send us the link and we'll tell you honestly whether it's worth fixing or rebuilding.

Want to see what your site could look like?

Email your business name and current website (if you have one), and we'll send you a free homepage mockup.
