The problems we fix

Does your website have any of these?

Most small business websites aren't bad on purpose. They were built years ago and nobody has had time to look after them. Here's what I see most often, and what I do about it.

01

Not mobile friendly

Tiny text, buttons you can't tap, pages you have to pinch and zoom. Most local searches happen on a phone, and people leave quickly if a site is hard to use.

→

FixA mobile-first design with big, clear call and enquiry buttons.

02

“Not secure” warning

Without HTTPS, browsers show "Not secure" next to your address. It puts customers off, and any form they fill in isn't encrypted.

Not secure
https://

FixHTTPS set up and checked, so the padlock shows and data is encrypted.

03

Outdated or hacked

Old software and plugins that never get updated are a common way sites get broken into, filled with spam or redirected somewhere dodgy.

FixA clean rebuild or clean-up, then regular updates and backups.

04

Broken forms & links

A contact form that quietly stopped sending, or links that go nowhere. You might be missing enquiries and never know it.

FixForms that are tested and deliver to your inbox, and links that work.

05

Slow pages

Huge images and heavy themes make pages crawl, especially on mobile data. People give up before your page even loads.

Heavy
Light

FixLightweight pages, compressed images and fast hosting.

06

Not showing on Google

Missing page titles and descriptions, no Search Console, no link to your Google Business Profile. Google has a harder time understanding what you do and where.

FixThe Google basics done properly on every page.

07

No online booking or quotes

If the only way to reach you is to call during business hours, you miss people who are browsing at night or on the weekend.

FixA simple booking request or quote form that sends straight to you.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Do I own my domain and my website?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your name and stays yours. The words, photos and content on your site are yours too. If you ever leave, I'll hand everything over.

I already have a website. Can you work with it?

Often, yes. Send me the link and I'll tell you honestly whether it's worth fixing or better to rebuild. The website tune-up is $199 one-off (for small sites; bigger jobs are quoted first), and $99/mo care is optional if you want me to keep it secure and updated, and if you later go for a full rebuild the $199 comes off the build price.

How long does it take?

About 2 weeks from when you say yes, as long as I have your logo, photos and feedback in reasonable time. Bigger Pro sites can take a little longer, and I'll tell you upfront.

What do you need from me?

Your logo (if you have one), 5 to 10 photos of your work, shop or team, a few answers about your business, and access to your domain so I can connect it. I write the words. Everything else is handled by me.

Can I edit the website myself?

Most clients prefer to just email changes through. Care plans include monthly edit time (30 minutes on Basic, 1 hour on Plus and Pro). If you'd like to edit some things yourself, mention it at the start and we'll talk through the options.

What happens if I cancel my care plan?

You keep your domain and your content. I'll give you your site files and help you move it to hosting of your choice. From then on, hosting, updates, backups and security are up to you.

The Business package's lower $490 build price comes with Care Plus for a minimum of 6 months. After that it's month-to-month: keep going, switch plans, or stop with 30 days' notice.

How much does hosting cost?

On any care plan, hosting is included. With a one-off Starter or Pro build and no care plan, you look after hosting yourself (I can point you to good options). Either way, your domain name renews yearly and is paid directly to the registrar in your name.

Will I be number one on Google?

No one can honestly guarantee Google rankings, and you should be wary of anyone who does. What I do is get the basics right: page titles and descriptions, a fast mobile site, Google Search Console, and a link to your Google Business Profile. That gives Google what it needs to understand what you do and where.

How do I pay?

50% deposit to start and 50% when your site goes live, by bank transfer. Care plans are billed monthly from launch. All prices are fixed and agreed in writing first.

What areas do you cover?

I'm based in Noble Park and work with businesses across south-east Melbourne, including Dandenong, Springvale, Keysborough, Clayton, Mulgrave, Narre Warren, Berwick and Cranbourne. Because everything can be done by email, I can help businesses further away too.
