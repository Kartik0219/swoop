Questions people ask first

How much does tiling cost in Melbourne?

It depends on the size of the room, the type of tiles, and the preparation needed, such as waterproofing, floor levelling and removing old tiles. We give a free, no-obligation quote made for your project.

Do you tile commercial properties?

Yes: offices, retail spaces, restaurants and other business premises. We can schedule the work so it disrupts your business as little as possible.

Can you fix cracked tiles without retiling everything?

Yes. Our renovation and repair service fixes chipped, cracked, loose or missing tiles, so you can restore the space without a complete retile.

What tiles are best for a swimming pool?

Pool tiles need to handle constant submersion and pool chemicals. Glass mosaics and porcelain are good choices for their durability and non-slip properties, and we can help you choose.
