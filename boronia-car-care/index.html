About us

Ron, Rommel and the team

Originally established in 2006 by Ron Kelaita, Boronia Car Care has been providing automotive repairs to our customers ever since. Ron has been in the industry for over 25 years, and works alongside his brother Rommel and one other mechanic.

Rommel is also a qualified panelbeater. We don't provide panelbeating services, but if you need some advice or have questions, we're happy to help.
