Our story

Two generations of electricians

We put a high emphasis on client liaison and service, which has let us keep working with clients from both generations. Several of these associations have been maintained for over 40 years.

Insurance and compliance are a high priority, and we can supply certification of that compliance to industry standards.

1965

The business is originally established.

1990

Renamed Mac Electrics (Aust) as the next generation of electricians progressively takes over.

Today

Office and workshop at 14 Maxwell Street, Dandenong, serving commercial and industrial clients across Melbourne.
