Services

Care for every stage of life

General, cosmetic and preventive dentistry in one friendly practice. For anything more specialised, we work with a number of excellent local dental specialists.

🦷

General dentistry

  • Tooth-coloured fillings
  • Root canal (endodontics)
  • Crowns & bridges
  • Dentures, full and partial
  • TMJ / jaw joint pain
  • Tooth erosion and wear
✨

Cosmetic dentistry

  • Veneers
  • Tooth whitening
  • Tooth-coloured restorations
  • Implant restoration
🛡️

Preventive care

  • Oral hygiene
  • Periodontal (gum) care
  • Fissure sealants
  • Sports mouthguards
  • Grinding splints
Children's dentistry

Good habits start early

We encourage children's check-ups from 12 months. We explain each step in words your child understands, pause whenever they need a break, and give plenty of praise along the way.

Book a family check-up
  • Dental exam and tooth count
  • Initial orthodontic assessment
  • Clean, polish and fluoride
  • Fissure sealants where needed
