Our story

Family-run, with plenty of practice

Stephen and Nancy, plus family and team, are all passionate about serving great food and coffee in a comfortable and cosy local café setting. Stephen's passion for food developed across many years, and six kids to feed ensured plenty of practice.

Today the team serves a hearty menu of breakfast, brunch, lunch and treats for all locals to enjoy.
