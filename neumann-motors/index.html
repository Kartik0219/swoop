About us

A family business with local roots

Adam, Phil and Linda make up the Neumann Motors team. We know the biggest concern for our customers is a fair price and an honest assessment from a mechanic they can trust, and that shapes how we work every day.

🏉 Proud local sponsor

Of the Pines Football Club and the Frankston RSL.

👨‍🔧 Skilled technicians

Over 20 years of experience, aiming to fix it right the first time.

1996

Neumann Motors is founded by Frank Neumann.

2000

Adam Marriner joins Frank as an apprentice.

13 years later

Adam and his step-dad Phil buy the business, carrying on Frank's high standards and ethical treatment of customers and their cars.

Today

Still family owned and operated, on Pascal Road in Seaford.
