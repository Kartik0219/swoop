FAQ

Good to know

Manual or automatic licence?

It's your choice, but with an automatic probationary licence that's the only type of car you can drive until your probation ends, unless you re-sit the test in a manual.

How many lessons will I need?

There's no set minimum or maximum. Your driver trainer will give you an assessment of how many you need after your first lesson.

Do I have to drive on busy roads in my first lesson?

Your instructor will be patient and can start you on quiet streets. Every lesson is adjusted to your level.
